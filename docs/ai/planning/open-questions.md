## Reverified corrections to the earlier map

* Its proposed Owner/Admin phase is superseded by D1; inward consignment remains out of scope, despite a suggested later batch in that map.
* Its margin criticism is incorrect for the supplied v0.3 example: ₦11,500 selling price at ₦9,400 cost gives **18.3% gross margin**, falling to **14.8%** at ₦9,800. The spec's “18% to 15%” is consistent with the [report revenue-denominator formula](../../../src/app/(dashboard)/reports/page.tsx). Use margin, not markup. The proposed ₦11,900 restores about 17.6%, approximately 18%.
* Invoice/PO example arithmetic checks: ₦431,300 PO, ₦473,600 invoice, ₦42,300 difference. These are illustrative fixtures, not market-price evidence. Original text/spec contents were preserved in the input commit; planning links resolve from this directory.
